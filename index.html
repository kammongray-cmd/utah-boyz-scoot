HOW TO RIDE

👈 LEFT THUMB

Flick UP = kick-push (hold up to keep pushing)

Left / Right = steer

Pull DOWN = foot brake

RIGHT THUMB 👉

Pull DOWN, then let go (or flick up) = bunny hop. Do it right at the lip of a ramp to boost higher.

In the air, hold LEFT/RIGHT = spin 180, 360, 540…

Hold DOWN = backflip · hold UP = front flip

Flick diagonals: ↙ Tailwhip · ↘ Barspin · ↖ Whiplash · ↗ Bri Flip. Flick again quickly for a double!

Land square (or fakie) and finish your tricks before you touch down. Land on rails and coping to grind.

Points unlock better scooters, new colors and harder tricks. Land tricks back-to-back for a LINE multiplier.
